# Lay out the garden beds and grow flowers

Draw the garden: five asymmetric flower beds (A–E per the README sketch) with broad
open walkways between them, plus flowers that grow on their own.

- Beds are pre-seeded with a few flower slots each.
- Each flower grows through visible stages (e.g. sprout → bud → bloom → mature)
  on a timer; stages are procedurally drawn (circles/curves, no assets).
- Mature flowers get a subtle pulsing halo — no permanent icons above them.
- Layout leaves the top HUD strip and bottom toolbar strip empty for later tickets.

## Acceptance criteria

- All five beds and their flowers are visible on the 360×640 viewport with clear
  negative space between beds.
- Flowers visibly progress through growth stages over time and end mature with a
  pulsing halo.
