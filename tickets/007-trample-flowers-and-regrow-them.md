# Trample flowers and regrow them

Close the threat loop: a unicorn that reaches its noticed flower tramples it.

- Trampled flower is visibly flattened, then automatically regrows from stage 0
  (no player planting action).
- After trampling, the unicorn returns to wandering (or leaves).
- A small feedback effect on trample (dust puff / squash) so losses are legible.

## Acceptance criteria

- A unicorn reaching a flower flattens it; the flower visibly regrows from stage 0
  over time.
- The unicorn resumes wandering afterwards.
